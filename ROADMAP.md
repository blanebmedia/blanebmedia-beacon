# Beacon Roadmap

## Vision

Beacon helps business owners answer five critical questions:

1. Where am I?
2. How do I compare?
3. Why is this happening?
4. What is my business worth?
5. What happens next?

Beacon begins as a business readiness platform and evolves into a predictive business intelligence platform capable of identifying patterns associated with business success.

---

## Phase 1 — Readiness Engine

**Core Question:** "Where am I today?"

**Objective:** Provide business owners with a clear assessment of operational readiness using the Beacon Framework.

### Features

**Business Profile**
- Business name
- Industry
- NAICS code
- ZIP code
- Team size
- Years in business
- Revenue range

**8 Business Systems Assessment**
- Administration
- Training
- Products
- Current Campaign
- Growth
- Logistics
- Marketing
- Finance

**Readiness Scoring**
- Emerging
- Operational
- Scalable
- Exit Ready

**Dashboard**
- Overall readiness score
- Strongest system
- Weakest system
- Recommended next action

**Founder Cohort**
- 100 beta businesses
- Founding member pricing
- Data collection initiative

**Success Metric:** Acquire and retain the first 100 businesses.

## Long-Term Strategic Asset

The true asset of Beacon is not the software.

The true asset is the proprietary dataset generated from thousands of businesses across industries.

This dataset enables Beacon to identify patterns between operational maturity, marketing effectiveness, financial performance, and long-term business success.

As the dataset grows, Beacon becomes increasingly capable of predicting business outcomes and recommending actions that improve business value.
